Nitrosomonas europaea, a bacterium involved in nitrification, uses the 3-hydroxypropionate/4-hydroxybutyrate (HPC/HB) pathway as its primary metabolic pathway for obtaining energy. This unique pathway allows the bacteria to perform carbon fixation and utilize ammonia as an energy source (Berg et al.). The HPC/HB pathway is a key adaptation that enables Nitrosomonas europaea to thrive in environments with limited organic carbon availability, such as wastewater treatment systems (Huang et al., 2018). This metabolic pathway is distinct from other nitrifying bacteria and highlights the unique characteristics of Nitrosomonas europaea.
