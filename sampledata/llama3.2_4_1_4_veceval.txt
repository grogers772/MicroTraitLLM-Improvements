In bread production, yeast species such as Saccharomyces cerevisiae (Sharma et al.) are commonly used for fermentation. Similarly, in beer production, yeasts like Pichia kluyveri and Saccharomyces cerevisiae (Huang et al., 2024) are utilized for co-fermentation to produce low-alcohol craft beer.

In the context of sourdough bread, microorganisms such as lactic acid bacteria and wild yeast species have been identified as contributing to the fermentation process (Reese et al., 2020). Additionally, a study on microbial fermentation in the food industry highlights the use of various microorganisms, including yeasts and molds, for fermentation purposes (Siddiqui et al., 2023).
