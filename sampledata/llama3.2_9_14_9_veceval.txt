According to the provided information, the fungus that turned out to be Penicillium notatum and produced the antibacterial molecule penicillin (Larsen et al., 2022) is a valuable source of natural therapeutic products such as antibiotics. The text also mentions that filamentous fungi are a valuable source of natural therapeutic products such as antibiotics, including penicillin (Fisher & Mobashery, 2023).
