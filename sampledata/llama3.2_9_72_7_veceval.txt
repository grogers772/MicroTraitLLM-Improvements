According to the provided article, the fungus that turned out to be Penicillium notatum and produced the antibacterial molecule penicillin (Lobanovska & Pilla, 2017) [1]. This discovery marked a significant milestone in the production of antibiotics. The remarkable accomplishment of industrial-scale production of penicillins from their original producing fungi may have contributed to the lack of interest in exploring other fungal sources for antibiotic production (Fisher & Mobashery, 2020) [2].

It is worth noting that Penicillium notatum was identified as the fungus responsible for producing penicillin through a process of engineering and experimentation with baker's yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae (Awan et al., 2017) [3]. This approach demonstrated the feasibility of using non-filamentous fungi to produce bioactive compounds, including antibiotics.

The discovery of Penicillium notatum as the source of penicillin has had a profound impact on modern medicine, transforming the treatment of bacterial infections and saving countless lives (Fisher & Mobashery, 2023) [4].
