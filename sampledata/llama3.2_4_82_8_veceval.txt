Based on the provided information, it appears that yeast is a common microorganism used in the production of bread and beer fermentation (Wang et al., 2025; Nasuti et al., 2023). Specifically, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, also known as baker's yeast, is commonly used in bread fermentation (Reese et al., 2020). In the context of beer fermentation, Pichia kluyveri and Saccharomyces cerevisiae are often co-fermented to produce low-alcohol craft beer (Huang et al., 2024).

Additionally, other microorganisms such as Corynebacterium glutamicum and Bacillus spp. are also used in fermentation processes, including bread production (Chen & Jiang). However, yeast is a dominant player in the production of fermented beverages like beer.

It's worth noting that while bacteria like E. coli and Bacillus spp. are mentioned as being used in fermentation processes, they are not typically associated with the production of bread or beer.
