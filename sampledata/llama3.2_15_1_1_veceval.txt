According to recent research, Listeria monocytogenes uses its virulence mechanisms to escape from the phagosome after being engulfed by a host cell. Specifically, the InlB protein of Listeria monocytogenes has been shown to expedite vacuole escape and intracellular proliferation by promoting Rab7 recruitment via Vps34 (Cain et al., 2023). This mechanism allows the bacteria to evade the phagosome's acidic environment and maintain its survival within the host cell. The InlB protein facilitates this process by recruiting Rab7, a small GTPase that regulates membrane trafficking, thereby promoting the escape of Listeria monocytogenes from the phagosome (Cain et al., 2023). This understanding of the bacterial mechanism is crucial for developing effective therapeutic strategies against listeriosis.
