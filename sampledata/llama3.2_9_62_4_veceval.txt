According to the provided article, the fungus that turned out to be Penicillium notatum and produced the antibacterial molecule penicillin (Lobanovska & Pilla, 2017) [1]. This discovery marked a significant milestone in the production of antibiotics. The article also highlights the remarkable accomplishment of industrial-scale production of penicillins from their original producing fungi, which may have contributed to the reduced interest in studying fungal sources for new antibiotics (Larsen et al., 2022) [2].

It is worth noting that Penicillium notatum is a species of fungus that has been extensively studied for its ability to produce penicillin, and it remains an important model organism for understanding the biosynthesis of this antibiotic.
