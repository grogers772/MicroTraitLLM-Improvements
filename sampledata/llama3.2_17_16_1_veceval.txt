According to Rhun et al. (2019), the CRISPR-Cas system plays a crucial role in Streptococcus pyogenes, serving as a defense mechanism against phage infection (Rhun et al., 2019). The study found that the CRISPR-Cas system is essential for the survival of S. pyogenes in response to phage infection. Additionally, Beerens et al. (2021) reported that anti-CRISPR proteins can trigger a burst of CRISPR-Cas9 expression, which enhances phage defense (Beerens et al., 2021). This suggests that the CRISPR-Cas system is not only involved in recognizing and responding to phages but also plays a role in regulating its own expression. Overall, the CRISPR-Cas system appears to be a vital component of S. pyogenes' defense strategy against phage infection.
